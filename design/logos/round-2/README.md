# Round 2: dialysis-focused directions

These are exploratory marks, not replacements for the live site logo. All four use FuelWell's current colors and can be paired with a plain `FuelWell` wordmark.

1. **Bean / kidney** (`01-bean-kidney.svg`) — a bean silhouette bridges the coffee-bean idea and kidney care. The curved center line suggests a bean seam or a path through treatment. Risk: by itself, it may read as a coffee brand.
2. **F/W ligature** (`02-fw-ligature.svg`) — a shared F stem becomes the first stroke of a W. This gives FuelWell a mark it can own even without the name. Risk: at very small sizes, the letters need enough space to stay legible.
3. **Care loop** (`03-care-loop.svg`) — a continuous ring for ongoing support, with a flowing line inside for treatment, food, and the care team. Risk: the inner line could be read as a heart monitor, so it is the least dialysis-specific.
4. **F/W bean** (`04-fw-bean.svg`, `04-fw-bean-wordmark.svg`) — the F and W share a stem inside an organic bean or kidney silhouette. This combines the most distinctive parts of ideas 1 and 2. Risk: the initials become intricate at favicon size, so the mark should be simplified for very small use.

The strongest next direction is to refine **F/W bean**, then test its legibility at navigation and favicon sizes.
