**Target Audience:** Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR)  
G3b(30 to 44\) and G4 (15-29)

**Objectives:** 

* *Close the access gap to renal nutrition expertise*. After a G3b or G4 diagnosis, physicians usually recommend dietary changes. But patients might struggle to adjust to the renal diet on their own. Our service connects them to that expertise to prevent further kidney degradation. People shouldn’t change their diet only after they score below 15 eGFR. 

* *Design user-friendly applications for elderly and caregivers(adult children/social workers).* NIH research shows that the median age of G3b-G4 patients is 77-80. Our service will be user-friendly and tailored to these elderly users. Additionally, we will also serve their adult children and caregivers, who may be in charge of managing meals and care decisions. 

* Renal diet aligning with cultural food. Many current nutritionists cater towards generic, western-style food. However, patients are more likely to accept the renal dietary change when it fits the foods they are familiar with.. We will adapt renal restrictions to a wide range of cultural and ethnic cuisines.
