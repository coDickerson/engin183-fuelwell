How it works

From the care plan to tonight’s dinner.

You already juggle appointments, medicines and your own job. FuelWell takes the diet guidance from your parent’s care team and turns it into meals you can actually cook.

Try the demo Get started

Three steps

Set it up once, then just cook.

It takes about two minutes to start. You can change anything later.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us about your parent

    Their kidney stage, the foods they love, and what would help most right now.

  2. Step 2

    Get meals that fit

    Daily meal ideas built around familiar dishes, with a clear potassium, phosphorus and sodium check.

  3. Step 3

    Share with family

    Invite a sibling with view-only access, and bring questions to the next care-team visit.

Your care team stays in charge

Support for their advice, not a replacement.

FuelWell is a student project. It helps you follow the plan your parent’s doctor or renal dietitian gives you. It doesn’t set limits or change medicines.

Read common questions

Good to bring to each visit

  1. 1
    Their latest lab results

    Potassium, phosphorus and eGFR, if the team shares them.

  2. 2
    Foods that feel like home

    The dishes they would miss most.

  3. 3
    What daily life looks like

    Who shops, who cooks, and how much energy they have.
