Kidney-friendly meals that still taste like home.

FuelWell plans kidney-friendly meals from the everyday food you already eat, like roast chicken, pasta, soups and rice, and can add the dishes from your family’s culture. It also keeps lab results, health records and a renal dietitian in one place. Join the waitlist to be one of our first users.

Also living with

We only use this to contact you about FuelWell. No spam, and we never sell your information. Health answers are optional and only help us plan.

  • Starts with everyday meals

    Weeknight staples like roast chicken, pasta, sandwiches and oatmeal, with small swaps for potassium, phosphorus and salt. Cook Cantonese, Mexican, Vietnamese, Filipino or your own tradition too.

  • Health records and dietitian support

    Keep lab results in one place, message a FuelWell renal dietitian or nutritionist, and share the plan with your own doctor and care team.

  • Made for kidney disease stages G3 and G4

    These are the years before dialysis, when everyday food choices matter most, especially alongside diabetes or high blood pressure. FuelWell helps you get meals right now, not only after dialysis starts.

  • Made for the whole family

    For patients, sons and daughters, and caregivers.
