Meal planning

Everyday meals, and the flavors they grew up with.

FuelWell starts with the everyday meals most families already cook, like roast chicken, pasta, sandwiches and oatmeal, and suggests small changes that fit kidney limits. A kidney diet also shouldn’t mean giving up the foods that connect your family to its culture, so you can add the traditions that feel like home, from Cantonese to Mexican to your own.

Cantonese home cooking, for example

Begin with what is already theirs.

In the demo, Fung’s week includes the comfort food she has cooked for decades, each with a simple nutrient check.

Breakfast

Chicken congee

Gentle and familiar. Portion and broth matter for sodium.

Side dish

Stir-fried greens

Bok choy with garlic. Blanching first can lower potassium.

Dinner

Soup and rice

Lighter soups and white rice can fit a phosphorus limit.

Food notes are general examples for the demo, not individual medical advice. Check changes with your parent’s care team.
