Meal planning

Keep the flavors they grew up with.

A kidney diet shouldn’t mean giving up the foods that connect your family to its culture. FuelWell supports meals from different cultural traditions, bringing your parent’s familiar flavors, food preferences, and cultural needs into meal planning around their health. We start with the dishes they already love and suggest small changes that fit their dietary limits.

Cantonese home cooking, for example

Begin with what is already theirs.

In the demo, Fung’s week includes the comfort food she has cooked for decades, each with a simple nutrient check.

Breakfast

Chicken congee

Gentle and familiar. Portion and broth matter for sodium.

Side dish

Stir-fried greens

Bok choy with garlic. Blanching first can lower potassium.

Dinner

Soup and rice

Lighter soups and white rice can fit a phosphorus limit.

Food notes are general examples for the demo, not individual medical advice. Check changes with your parent’s care team.
