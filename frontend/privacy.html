Privacy Policy

Your family’s information, handled with care.

What we collect, who can see it, and how to get a copy or delete it.

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version: we collect what FuelWell needs to suggest kidney-friendly swaps for your family. Only you, and family members you invite, can see your profile. We never sell your information or show ads. Email us any time to get a copy or have everything deleted.

Who we are

FuelWell is run by a team of UC Berkeley students as part of ENGIN 183. It is not a company, clinic, doctor or health plan. Questions about this policy go to kongmegan_@berkeley.edu.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email and password. Phone number and your story only if you choose to add them. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, and we never see them.
  • About the person you cook for: what you choose to tell us, such as who they are to you, their CKD stage or eGFR, other conditions (high blood pressure, diabetes), the sodium, potassium, phosphorus or fluid limits from their care team, foods they usually eat, and the cuisines you cook.
  • Your questions: the meals and questions you type into Ask FuelWell, the answers it gives, and any photo you add. We keep your chat questions and answers so you can see your history. Photos are used to write the answer and are not saved by FuelWell.
  • What FuelWell remembers: when you tap “We’ll try it” or “Not for us” on a swap, and the dishes you ask about. FuelWell uses your newest few notes to tailor later answers. They are preferences, never medical facts. You can see and delete each one under My plan → What FuelWell remembers.
  • Answer feedback: when you mark an answer helpful or not, we save your rating, the reason you picked, your question and the answer’s title. The team reads these to find and fix weak answers.
  • Family sharing: the email address of anyone you invite, and the records you share with them.
  • Forms: what you enter in the waitlist, feedback or survey forms. Dishes sent to the dish wall are shown publicly, without your name.
  • How the site is used: anonymous counts, such as pages opened and buttons used, linked to a random ID stored in your browser and not to your name. We also use Vercel’s privacy-friendly visit statistics, which do not use cookies.

How we use it

  • To run your account and tailor swaps, recipes and answers to the plan from your family’s care team.
  • To answer your questions and emails, and to let you know about important changes to FuelWell.
  • To understand what helps families and improve FuelWell. Any results we share, for example in class, are totals that never identify you.

We never sell your information. We never use it for ads, and we never share it with insurers, employers or data brokers.

Who can see it

  • You, and family members you invite (they see only what you share).
  • A few FuelWell team members with database access. They look only when needed to run the service, fix a problem or answer your request.
  • Services that run FuelWell for us:
    • Supabase: database and sign-in, on servers in the United States.
    • Vercel: website hosting and visit statistics.
    • Anthropic: the Claude AI that writes answers. Your question, any photo, and the profile details needed for the answer are sent to Anthropic to write it. Anthropic’s commercial terms say it does not use this data to train its models.
    • Google Fonts, Wikimedia Commons, Pexels and Unsplash: they serve the site’s fonts and photos, so they can see your device’s internet address when a page loads.
  • The law: if we are legally required to share something, we share only what is required.

Health information

FuelWell isn’t a doctor, clinic or health plan. That means health privacy laws such as HIPAA may not cover what you tell us. We still treat it as sensitive:

  • Your profile and chat history are locked so that only your account can read them.
  • Everything travels over encrypted connections.
  • You can delete it at any time.

If you enter details about someone else, such as a parent, please make sure they’re comfortable with it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while you have an account. To get a copy, correct something, or delete your account and everything in it, email kongmegan_@berkeley.edu from the email you signed up with. We’ll do it within 30 days. Deleted data may stay in backups for a short time until they are replaced.

If the FuelWell project ends, we’ll email you before deleting the accounts.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules that keep each account’s data private, and sign-in through Supabase. No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that affects your information, we’ll tell you promptly.

Age

FuelWell is for adults 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If it’s a big change, we’ll email account holders before it takes effect.
