Common questions

Before you begin.

FuelWell is a student product demo. Here is what you can expect today.

Who is FuelWell for?

We’re designing it for adults with G3b or G4 kidney disease, along with adult children and caregivers involved in meals and care.

Can a family member make an account?

Yes. The demo lets you begin as a patient or caregiver and explore a sample shared care experience.

Can I book a dietitian today?

Appointment scheduling is planned, but it is not available in this demo. The quiz shows a possible next step.

Does the meal plan replace medical advice?

No. Sample content is for exploring the product. Individual diet decisions should be discussed with a qualified clinician familiar with your care.

Does insurance cover FuelWell?

Coverage has not been announced. We will share verified insurance and pricing information as the service develops.
