Terms of Use

Plain terms, no surprises.

The rules for using FuelWell, and what it is and isn’t.

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version: FuelWell is a free student project that helps families cook kidney-friendly versions of the food they love. It is not medical advice. Always follow the plan from your family’s doctor and dietitian.

About FuelWell

FuelWell is run by a team of UC Berkeley students as part of ENGIN 183. It is free. Because it is a student project, features may change, pause or end. We’ll give account holders notice by email before we shut it down.

Not medical advice

  • FuelWell gives general food information, based on published kidney-diet guidelines and recipes. It does not diagnose, treat or replace a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist.
  • AI answers can be wrong or incomplete. Check any change to diet, fluids, medicines or dialysis with the care team first.
  • Nutrient numbers are estimates. Real amounts depend on brands, portions and cooking.
  • In an emergency, call 911. Don’t use FuelWell for urgent symptoms.

Your account

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.
  • Give accurate information and keep your password private. You’re responsible for what happens in your account.
  • If you enter information about someone else, such as a parent you cook for, you need their permission or the right to act for them.
  • You can stop using FuelWell and ask us to delete your account at any time (see the Privacy Policy).

Using FuelWell fairly

Please don’t:

  • misuse the site or try to break into it
  • access other people’s accounts
  • overload the AI with automated requests
  • post anything unlawful, hateful or someone else’s private information

We may suspend accounts that do.

Your content

What you enter (profiles, questions, stories, photos) stays yours. You let us store and process it only to run FuelWell for you, as described in the Privacy Policy. If you send a dish to the public dish wall, you agree it can be shown there without your name.

Other people’s content

Recipes come from public sources such as the U.S. National Institutes of Health, and each one is credited. Photos are credited to their photographers. Links to other websites are for convenience. We don’t control those sites.

No guarantees

FuelWell is provided “as is”. We work hard to make it accurate and available, but we can’t promise it will always be correct, uninterrupted or error-free. As far as the law allows, the FuelWell team isn’t liable for decisions made based on FuelWell. That’s why the care team should always have the final word.

Changes and contact

If we update these terms, we’ll change the date at the top, and we’ll email account holders about important changes. These terms are governed by California law. Questions: kongmegan_@berkeley.edu.
