What FuelWell does

Meals you can cook, and care you can reach.

Two things matter most for kidney health at home: what goes on the plate, and staying in close touch with the people who guide the diet.

01 · Meal planning

A week of meals built from the food they already eat

  • Start from the dishes your family already cooks, from steamed fish and congee to roast chicken and pasta.
  • Every dish gets a simple potassium, phosphorus and sodium check.
  • Small swaps keep favorite dishes on the table, plus a grocery list for the week.

02 · Health records and care team

Labs, records and a dietitian, all in one place

  • Keep lab results like eGFR, potassium and phosphorus together, with trends in plain language.
  • Message a FuelWell renal dietitian who speaks your family’s language, and book a visit when questions come up.
  • Share the meal plan and labs with your own doctor, dietitian and care team.
