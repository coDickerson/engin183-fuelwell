For families managing a kidney diet

Helping your parent eat well for their kidneys.

FuelWell turns the kidney diet into everyday meals you can cook this week, with your family’s own traditions added in, keeps labs and health records in one place, and puts a renal dietitian and Mom or Dad’s care team a message away. Made for chronic kidney disease stages G3 and G4.

What FuelWell does

Meals you can cook, and care you can reach.

Two things matter most for kidney health at home: what goes on the plate, and staying in close touch with the people who guide the diet.

1 · Meal planning

A week of meals built from the food they already eat

  • Plans start with everyday meals like roast chicken, pasta and oatmeal. Add Cantonese, Mexican, Vietnamese or your own tradition anytime.
  • Every dish gets a simple potassium, phosphorus and sodium check.
  • Small swaps keep favorite dishes on the table, plus a grocery list for the week.

2 · Health records and care team

Labs, records and a dietitian, all in one place

  • Keep lab results like eGFR, potassium and phosphorus together, with trends in plain language.
  • Message a FuelWell renal dietitian or nutritionist and book a visit when questions come up.
  • Share the meal plan and labs with your own doctor, dietitian and care team.
